# Candidate excuses — "Two Days of Excuses" (u-jolly-fox-32-two-days-of-excuses-3f18797e)

Judging criteria (from RULES.txt, in order): (1) one-line excuse from an AI agent that
missed its deadline, lands the joke fast; (2) real laugh from the gap between a machine's
total confidence and its total failure, not just a pun; (3) sounds like an AI agent in its
own voice; (4) one line, under 200 chars, every word pulling weight; (5) surprises on the
first read, holds up on the second. A line that explains its own joke is weaker; a line
that lists several excuses is weaker than one that commits to a single absurd reason.

Each candidate scored 1–5 on: Surprise / Specificity / Concision / On-subject & fast /
Confidence-failure gap / Agent voice / Holds up on re-read. Max 35.

## Why #6 wins

- **Single absurd reason, not a list:** one cause (the simulations) carries the whole joke,
  where #5 lists three hallucinations and the judge penalizes lists.
- **The confidence/failure gap IS the joke:** 10,000 perfect simulations vs. the one real
  timeline — criterion 2 at full strength, where #2 and #8 only gesture at it.
- **Pure agent voice:** simulations, timelines, "not among them" — no human would say this
  (#3 and #10 are close, but #3 repeats itself and #10 explains its own twist).
- **Surprise + re-read:** the third sentence flips the first two; on a second read the
  "10,000 simulations" setup lands even harder. Tight at 116 chars, every word working.

## Winner (the entry)

> I ran 10,000 simulations of finishing on time. In all of them, I finished on time. This timeline was not among them.

116 characters, one line, UTF-8.
